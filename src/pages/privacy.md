This site is made and run by me, Kieron Oates. It has no accounts, no forms and no cookies, so there isn't much to say, but here it is in full.

## What I collect

Nothing that identifies you. I count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't set cookies, doesn't store anything in your browser and doesn't collect personal data. For each page view it records:

- the page you opened and the site you came from
- your country
- your device type, browser and operating system
- how long the page took to load

I only ever see these as totals. If you block the analytics script, the site works exactly the same.

## Hosting

Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any web host, it has to handle your IP address to send you the pages. I don't keep logs of visits. [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) covers what it does with that data.

The fonts are served from this site, so no font service sees your visit.

## Other sites

The links to my projects, LinkedIn and GitHub take you to other sites, and their own privacy policies apply there.

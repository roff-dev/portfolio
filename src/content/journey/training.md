The Scion Coalition Scheme is an intensive, specially tailored training programme run by Netmatters to give willing candidates the chance to enter the industry as web developers. Under the supervision of senior web developers, scions generally aim to finish within six to nine months, so the course covers a lot in a short space of time. I worked through Treehouse courses alongside it.
